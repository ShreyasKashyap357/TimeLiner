Hastings explains that she has come to tell him that his mother's demon helped a nurse and two patients escape Saint Teresa's the night before and that a guard was killed. She requests his help, since he is the only one who can talk to the demon, and Helstrom offers to pay the guard's funeral costs.[48][3]

  • Ana Helstrom arrives at work, where Yen tells her that he has successfully kept the auction out of the news, but complains she is being reckless. She finds she has a missed call from Caretaker, and Yen explains he also visited, having mentioned a crib in Colma.[48][3]
  • At Saint Teresa's, Daimon Helstrom and Hastings are joined by Gabriella Rosetti. Hastings shows them the security footage of Spivey's escape. Helstrom decides to visit his "mother", and Rosetti insists on joining so she can report to the Vatican, but he refuses her company.[48][3]
  • Helstrom enters his "mother's" cell, where he notices the symbol she has drawn on the wall and tries to talk to her. He attempts to jolt his mother's memory with a story of her kindness from his childhood. She appears to respond, but it is the demon taunting him. The demon claims Helstrom's memories are tainted by sentiment, and that his mother had tried to abort him by drinking. He inquires about the events of the night before and she eventually says she sent Keith Spivey and the patients to see "an old friend", before declaring the conversation to be finished.[48][3]

  • Ana Helstrom visits Caretaker in the Colma cemetery on his request, where he explains he did not know about the hidden cave until the night before. She asks where he has been, and he explains he has been in Texas, tracking something,[50][51] then takes her to the catacombs.[48][3]
  • As Helstrom and Caretaker investigate, they see the remains of the people who came the night before. Caretaker shows her the sarcophagus, which is broken open with a skeleton inside with only one eye - ancient and demonic. Caretaker identifies it as a Keeper, and they wonder whether it could have been keeping Helstrom's father at bay. She nonetheless refuses to reach out to her brother. Caretaker warns Helstrom not to touch anything, but she decides to take the Keeper's Skull.[48][3]
  • At the Saint Teresa Center for Mental Health, Daimon Helstrom tells Gabriella Rosetti and Louise Hastings that he knows the symbol in Victoria's room was demonic in nature, and tells them about the mention of an "old friend". Rosetti wants to go in alone to verify her behavior is real, Helstrom snaps about her poor judgement with cases like Archer Cavallo. She says she has a report to write to the archbishop, and tells Helstrom she does not care for his disrespect. After she leaves, Helstrom admits to Hastings that he knows he is being rude, and Hastings reminds him they need Rosetti's help. Helstrom admits his frustration at being unable to help his mother and ponders asking for his sister's help, though considers reaching out likely pointless.[48][3]
  • Rosetti contacts the bishop of the archdiocese with an update about Saint Teresa's and Helstrom.[48][3]

Hastings explains how Daimon uses happy childhood memories to briefly coax Victoria out, suggesting this to Ana, but instead Ana slaps her mother. The demon reacts by telekinetically throwing the women back and into the wall.[48][49]

However, she works out what he was saying and an argument begins. As they lose control of their abilities, items start shaking, and "Victoria" senses their argument in her cell.
